Why this matters

Retirement isn’t one decision

It’s no longer a single, one-off event. People retire early, move to part-time work, or never fully stop — and every path is shaped by things beyond the numbers, from health to family responsibilities.

Most of us are unprepared

Few people have an in-depth understanding of pensions, or how to make savings last 30 years or more. Decisions often get made late, without enough time to weigh up the options.

AI can help — if you ask well

A generic question to an AI chatbot gets a generic answer. Asking a well-structured, informed question — with context about your own situation — gets you something far more useful to actually act on.

This isn’t financial advice

It’s a way to think more clearly and arrive better prepared — whether that’s for your own research or your next conversation with a qualified financial adviser.

Three levels of support

Each level builds on the last. Use whichever fits how much time you have — you can always come back and go further.

Level 1

Don’t know where to start?

If you’re not sure what to ask yet, this single all-purpose prompt gets an AI to ask you the right clarifying questions and walk you through the essentials.

Level 2

Choose from a list of questions

Pick from a curated list of expert-written prompts covering the foundations of a plan, things to watch out for, and how to get the most from your pension.

Level 3

Personalise it to you

Select your questions first, then add details about your age, savings, income sources and concerns — so the answer speaks to your actual circumstances.
