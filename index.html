Why this matters

Retirement isn’t one decision

It’s no longer a single, one-off event. People retire early, move to part-time work, or never fully stop — and every path is shaped by things beyond the numbers, from health to family responsibilities.

Most of us are unprepared

Few people have an in-depth understanding of pensions, or how to make savings last 30 years or more. Decisions often get made late, without enough time to weigh up the options.

More of us are using AI

It’s increasingly common to turn to an AI chatbot with questions about pensions and income. But a generic question gets a generic answer. This tool gives you focused, UK-specific questions so what comes back is actually useful.

This isn’t financial advice

It’s a way to think more clearly and arrive better prepared — whether that’s for your own research or your next conversation with a qualified financial adviser.

Important information about this site

We only help you ask better questions. This tool doesn’t calculate anything or give you answers itself — it builds a clear, UK-focused prompt for you to use with an AI chatbot of your choice.

AI answers aren’t guaranteed to be right. Chatbots can get facts wrong, use outdated figures, or miss details specific to your situation — even when given a good question. Always double-check anything that matters.

This isn’t financial advice. Nothing produced by this tool, or by the AI chatbots it links to, is regulated financial advice. It shouldn’t be the basis for decisions like changing your pension, retiring, or moving your savings.

You aren’t protected if a chatbot gets it wrong. AI chatbots are not regulated financial advisers. If you act on an answer and lose money, you can’t take a complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service or claim from the Financial Services Compensation Scheme.

Free, impartial help is available. MoneyHelper (opens in a new tab) offers free guidance on pensions, and its Pension Wise service gives a free appointment to people aged 50 or over with a defined contribution pension.

Always speak to a professional. For anything that affects your retirement income, talk to a regulated, independent financial adviser who can look at your full circumstances before you act. You can check a firm or adviser on the FCA Register (opens in a new tab).

Watch out for scams. Be wary of anyone who pressures you to move your pension, promises guaranteed returns or contacts you out of the blue. Take your time, and check the firm on the FCA Register first.

Your privacy. We don’t store or track what you type, and this site sets no cookies. We count how often each part of the tool is used, for example how many prompts are copied. The counts hold nothing about you or what you typed. When you launch a chatbot, your prompt — including any details you added — is sent to that chatbot’s provider and handled under their privacy policy. Don’t include your name, address, National Insurance number or account numbers.

We don’t endorse any chatbot. The chatbots listed are widely used examples. The Consumer Duty Alliance does not recommend one over another.
