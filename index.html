Getting started
I am [age] years old and want to retire at [target age]. In simple terms, what are the key things I should be thinking about right now to make sure I have enough income in retirement?
What is a realistic monthly income I should aim to have in retirement, and what are the main ways people fund that income?
What are the most common mistakes people make when planning for retirement income, and how can I avoid them?
Income planning
What are the different ways I can turn my pension savings into a retirement income — such as drawdown, annuity, or a combination? What are the pros and cons of each?
How do I work out how much of my pension pot I can safely withdraw each year without running out of money? What is the "safe withdrawal rate" and does it apply to me?
If I have both a defined benefit (final salary) pension and a defined contribution pension, how should I think about using them together to create a reliable income?
What role should an annuity play in my retirement income plan? At what point does buying an annuity make more sense than keeping money invested?
Tax efficiency
How can I take income from my pension and other savings in retirement in a tax-efficient way? What is the best order to draw down different accounts?
What tax-free allowances and reliefs are available to retirees in the UK? How can I make sure I am using them fully each year?
How does taking my pension tax-free lump sum affect my overall retirement income plan? Should I take it all at once or in stages?
Pension drawdown
What is pension drawdown and how does it work? What are the biggest risks I need to manage if I go into drawdown?
What is "sequence of returns risk" in pension drawdown and what strategies exist to protect against it?
How should my investment strategy change as I move from accumulation (saving) to decumulation (drawing income)? What type of portfolio is appropriate in drawdown?
State pension & benefits
How does the UK State Pension work, how much can I expect to receive, and how does my National Insurance record affect it?
Does it make financial sense to defer my State Pension? What are the break-even points and when would deferral benefit me?
Care costs & longevity
What are the potential costs of long-term care in later life, and how can I plan financially to cover them without it devastating my retirement income?
How do I plan for the possibility of living much longer than I expect — say to 95 or 100 — without running out of money?
